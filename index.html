type race / race a friend by typing

2 players · same words · one track

Type fast.
Drive fast.

Race a friend in real time. Your typing speed is your car's speed.

How it plays

  1. 1

    Open a room

    Pick a name and create a room. You're the host.

  2. 2

    Send the link

    Your friend opens it on their own device. Start unlocks when they join.

  3. 3

    Type to drive

    You both get the same words. Type faster, go faster.

Speed is your WPM

Measured over the last three seconds, with momentum, so bursts help but can't spike.

Mistakes cost acceleration

Wrong letters show red and you keep going, but the car picks up speed slower for a moment.

Clean streaks give nitro

Eight words in a row without a mistake fire a short boost.
