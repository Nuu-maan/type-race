Race a friend in real time. Your typing speed is your car's speed.

How it plays

  1. 1

    Open a room

    Pick a name and create a room. You're the host.

  2. 2

    Send the link

    Up to four friends join from their own devices. Start unlocks at two racers.

  3. 3

    Type to drive

    Everyone gets the same words. Type faster, go faster.

Speed is your WPM

Measured over the last three seconds, with momentum.

Mistakes cost acceleration

Wrong letters show red and you keep going, but you pick up speed slower.

Clean streaks give nitro

Eight words in a row without a mistake fire a short boost.

Level up your car

Every race earns XP, more for a podium finish. New paints and bodies unlock as you level.
